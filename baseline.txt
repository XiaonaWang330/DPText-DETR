Loss 体系
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

匹配: Hungarian Matcher (点级匹配，基于 cls score + coord L1)
匹配后 indices → 确定哪些 query 对应哪个 GT

Decoder 损失:
├─ loss_ce: Focal Loss (每个控制点的二分类: text/not-text)
│    输入: pred_logits (B,100,16,1) vs targets
│    权重: POINT_CLASS_WEIGHT
│
└─ loss_ctrl_points: L1 Loss (控制点坐标回归)
     输入: pred_ctrl_points (B,100,16,2) vs targets
     权重: POINT_COORD_WEIGHT

Encoder 损失:
├─ loss_ce: Focal Loss (编码器proposal分类)
├─ loss_bbox: L1 Loss (bbox回归)
└─ loss_giou: GIoU Loss (bbox质量)

AUX Loss: 前5层decoder + encoder → 各乘相同权重
